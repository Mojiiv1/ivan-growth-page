Good to know

Before your visit.

Can I book online?

Ivan has an official booking page. Availability and the full booking flow have not been verified for this demo. Calling your chosen location is the clearest way to confirm an appointment.

Are the same services available at both salons?

Ivan’s services page says offerings differ by location. Confirm the service and current price with the branch you plan to visit.

Is there a dedicated space for hijab clients?

Ivan’s official website lists a dedicated space. Please call to confirm the location and arrangements before your visit.
