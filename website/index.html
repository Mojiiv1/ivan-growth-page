GOOD TO KNOW

Before your appointment

How do I book?

Use Ivan's current booking page or call your preferred salon. Online booking may offer multiple services in one appointment.

Is there a private section for hijab clients?

Ivan's original site lists a dedicated section. Call the salon to confirm the location and arrangements before booking.

What is the cancellation policy?

Ivan's original website requests at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations. Confirm the current policy with the salon.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Yes. Ivan's website encourages clients to bring inspiration photographs to a consultation.
