# Ivan / Tap & Grow design system

Editorial salon identity: warm ivory canvas, near-black framing, original gold logo, fine rules, large serif typography and authentic full-colour photography. The composition uses offset photographs and open service rows rather than SaaS cards, gradients or glass effects. System Georgia and sans-serif avoid external font dependencies and layout shifts.

Home → Services → Bridal → Gallery → Locations/Contact. No About page: no verified business story available. Home introduces the work, service categories, privacy provision and both locations. Services links to branch-specific official menus. Bridal provides only verified Hemlock starting prices and a phone enquiry. Gallery presents all five supplied photographs. Locations pairs each phone, address, directions and published schedule.

Conversion: Call to book → select location; mobile bar offers Call to book and Find us. On the contact page, calls go directly to the chosen branch. The official booking page is a secondary external action; no fake appointment form. Google reviews stay an optional text link, not the lead action.

Light is the default editorial palette; Dark is an explicit persisted preference. The black header/footer and natural photographs remain constant. Accent text and buttons use separate contrast-aware gold tokens. Theme storage failures cannot stop navigation or photo viewing. No animated colour transitions.

Improvements over the reference: no-JS navigation and photo links; shared generation eliminates repeated-header drift; full-resolution lightbox uses the original asset rather than currentSrc; modal body scroll lock and focus restoration; deterministic build; nested-path integrity and no-JS tests; single provenance register; clear separation between verified source facts and unconfirmed production details.

Motion is limited to subtle hover feedback and respects reduced motion. No auto-playing gallery, parallax, scroll reveal that hides content or decorative loading sequence.
