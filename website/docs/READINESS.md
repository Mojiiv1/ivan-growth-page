# Demo and launch readiness

This is a Tap & Grow Ottawa design demo, not the official Ivan website. The header and footer identify the demo. All five pages carry noindex,nofollow; no analytics, lead capture or business JSON-LD is installed.

Before production:
- Confirm current phone numbers and schedules with both branches; specifically Hemlock Tuesday's published noon closing and conflicting third-party Woodroffe details.
- Confirm current branch-specific services and starting prices, taxes and surcharges. The demo only displays the two clearly sourced Hemlock bridal starting prices.
- Test the complete official booking flow, branch selection, availability and cancellation behaviour with the salon; do not make a test appointment without permission.
- Confirm which branch offers the dedicated space for hijab clients and how to request it.
- Approve logo/photo publication rights and client consent; obtain actual bridal portfolio photos if desired.
- Confirm official domain, deployment target and owner approval. Remove demo labels/noindex only for an approved production launch, then add canonical URLs, sitemap and verified business structured data.
- Complete physical-device, screen-reader and native browser zoom checks. Automated QA is not certification.

Unknowns are deliberately not filled with invented values. No reviews, booking slots, ownership story, bridal photo claim or unsupported services have been added.
